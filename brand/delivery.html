The voice was never the problem. Take 1 says "you cannot stop them" and "you do not lose" — nobody speaks like that, so it comes out sounding like a form letter being read aloud. Takes 2 and 4 use contractions, break the lines apart so there is somewhere to breathe, and turn one statement into a question so the emphasis lands on its own.

Take 4 goes further: your key has the v3 model, which reads stage directions written into the text. That means the delivery can be directed line by line — dry here, amused there — instead of hoping one setting fits all fourteen lines.
