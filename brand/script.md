# SPOT — launch video script

~35 seconds. Every line is timed to something happening to the **O**.

| # | Voice over | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | This is SPOT. | wordmark builds, the **O** is an empty acid ring |
| 2 | Every inch of this page is for sale. | survey ticks snap onto the letters, prices appear |
| 3 | You buy a spot. You put whatever you want in it. | the **O** fills with a picture |
| 4 | Your face. Your coin. Your dog. | the **O** flicks through three pictures, fast |
| 5 | But here is the thing. | everything stops. the O holds one picture |
| 6 | Anyone can take it off you. | a second cursor slides in and clicks the **O** |
| 7 | For one and a half times what you paid. | price ticks up 250K → 375K |
| 8 | You cannot stop them. | the picture in the **O** is replaced, hard cut |
| 9 | But you do not lose. Ninety five percent of that goes straight to you. | tokens fly from the O to a wallet, counter runs up |
| 10 | The rest burns. Gone. | 5% chars and disappears, supply counter drops |
| 11 | And whoever claims a spot first? All of it burns. | 100% burns, the whole number goes to ash |
| 12 | Even this O is for sale. | the **O** in the wordmark gets a price tag |
| 13 | So is the cursor. Really. | the pointer itself turns into a picture |
| 14 | SPOT. Every inch is for sale. | full wordmark, ticker, CA |

## Notes on the jokes

- **4** is the fast one — three pictures in under a second, the last one absurd.
- **6** is the whole pitch in one shot: you watch the thing get taken while
  the voice is still talking about owning it.
- **12–13** are the wink. The site really does sell its own O and its own
  cursor, so the joke is true, which is why it lands.

## Delivery

Flat and deadpan. The lines are already the joke; performing them kills it.
A robotic voice suits this — it sounds like a system announcing a rule, not a
person selling something.
